In Memoriam

Husband. Father. Apostle. Statesman.

Apostle Prosper OgheneChovwen Sunday Okujere was born on 17th August 1961 in Ekakpamre, Delta State. Over six decades he built a life that embodied God's grace: as a devoted civil servant for thirty-one years, a loving patriarch who led his family with wisdom and sacrifice, and an apostle who sparked prayer fires across Delta State.

He is remembered as "The Fasting Apostle" — a man whose consecration to God was as uncompromising as his compassion for people. He founded the Divine Purpose Fasting and Prayer Camp and the Sound of Abundance Ministry, leaving behind living institutions that carry his fire forward.

"Until resurrection day, rest on, great eagle."
