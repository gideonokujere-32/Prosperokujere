Enduring Impact

The Legacy

The deep footprints of selfless generosity, prayer devotion, and generational empowerment Apostle Prosper Okujere left behind.

Four Pillars

What He Left Behind

A life measured not by its length but by the indelible marks of love, prayer, and service it left on every heart it touched.

🕯️

The Culture of Fasting & Consecration

Pioneering the 7-day annual Divine Purpose Fasting and Prayer Camp — inspiring thousands to cultivate authentic, uncompromising communion with God every December.

✨

Lifting Shining Stars

He lived by his creed: "Never be the only shining star in the sky of your world." Using his position, resources, and influence to educate and employ countless relatives, youths, and friends.

🏛️

Integrity in Governance

Three decades of dedicated civil service to the Government of Delta State — demonstrating that a public officer can remain godly, honest, kind, and excellent.

🌱

Kingdom Church Planting

Founding Sound of Abundance Ministry in Osubi — nurturing believers into spiritual maturity, doctrinal steadfastness, and practical love.

The Axiom He Lived By
❝
He never believed in being the only shining star in the sky of his world. He wanted more shining stars alongside him in the firmament.
— The Creed of Apostle Prosper OgheneChovwen Sunday Okujere
